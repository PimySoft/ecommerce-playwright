# Removing one product keeps the others

### Summary
Removing a product from a cart with several products removes only that product

### User story
As a shopper, I want to remove one product from my cart, so that I keep the rest of my order.

### Acceptance criteria
- Given a cart holding Blue Top and Men Tshirt, when Blue Top is removed, then Men Tshirt is still in the cart and Blue Top is not
- A product in the cart shows its unit price

### Priority
Medium

### Component
Cart
