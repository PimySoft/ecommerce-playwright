# Edit quantity in the cart

### Summary
Shoppers can change a product's quantity in the cart

### User story
As a shopper, I want to change how many of a product I'm buying from the cart, so that I don't have to add it again.

### Acceptance criteria
- Changing a product's quantity in the cart to 3 updates its line total to three times its price

### Priority
Medium

### Component
Cart
